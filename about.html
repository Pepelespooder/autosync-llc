OUR STORY

Why Border City Key & Tune
got started.

Border City Key & Tune started after I was quoted $1,000 for a single automotive job. That experience pushed me to start my own business and look for a better way to serve local drivers.

My goal is to offer clear conversations about the work needed and pass savings on to customers where I can. I work with car keys, ECU and PCM programming, and vehicle tuning.

Every vehicle and job is different. Reach out with your vehicle details and the issue you’re facing, and we can discuss what makes sense for your situation.
